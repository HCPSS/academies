Pathway Options

Computer Networking Pathway

Students will complete coursework and take the CompTIA Net+ and have the option to take the CISCO Certified Network Administrator (CCNA) and the CompTIA Security + certification exams.

Cyber Ops

Students will complete coursework and take the Comp TIA IT Fundamentals and A+ certification exams. Students will also have the option to take the Net+ certification exams.

Students will have the option of completing a worksite experience in a computer repair or networking field
