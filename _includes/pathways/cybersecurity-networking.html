Pathway Options

Computer Networking Pathway

Students have the opportunity to sit for the Cisco Certified Support Technician (CCST) and have the option to take the CompTIA Net+ and CISCO Certified Network Administrator (CCNA) certification exams.

Cyber Ops

Students have the opportunity to sit for the CompTIA A+ and Net+ certification exams. Students will have the option of completing a worksite experience in a computer repair or networking field
